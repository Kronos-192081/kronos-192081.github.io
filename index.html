I completed an Integrated Bachelor and Masters in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, where I worked on problems associated with usable security and privacy, advised by Mainack Mondal

My research interests lie in usable security and privacy _ understanding security and privacy challenges in real-world systems and user-facing technologies. I have worked on problems spanning software security, security issue reporting, and user decision-making in digital payment systems, and I am broadly interested in human-centric security and privacy at the intersection of users, software, and security.
